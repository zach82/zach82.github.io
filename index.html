Zaipeng Xie

Associate Professor · Hohai University

Multi-agent reinforcement learningFederated and distributed learningEfficient AI systems

I am an Associate Professor in the College of Computer Science and Software Engineering at Hohai University, China. I received my B.Eng. in Information Engineering from Southeast University, Nanjing, China, and my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, USA. Between 2009 and 2015, I was a visiting scholar at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, USA.

My research centers on multi-agent reinforcement learning, particularly coordination, communication, and robustness. I also work on federated learning, distributed computing, and efficient AI systems. I have authored more than 50 papers and am an inventor on more than 20 granted patents.

I also serve as an Associate Editor of Sustainable Computing: Informatics and Systems and an Editorial Board Member of Scientific Reports.

Research

Multi-agent reinforcement learning

Coordination, communication, and robust adaptation in teams of agents.

Federated and distributed learning

Learning across devices with heterogeneous data and models.

Efficient AI systems

Efficient neural architectures, spiking models, and resource-aware mixture-of-experts inference.
